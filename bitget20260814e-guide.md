## 3. 📊 How to Pick a Reliable Trader (This Makes or Breaks Copy Trading)

### 3.1 Must-Check Core Metrics

- 📈 **Track record returns**: Look at 30-day/90-day/180-day returns — favor long-term consistency over short-term explosions
- 🎯 **Win rate**: 60%–80% is the healthy zone; a 100% win rate is actually suspicious (likely ultra-short holds or pure luck)
- 📉 **Max drawdown**: Smaller is better for risk control — anything over 30% warrants caution
- 👥 **Follower count and copied capital**: More followers means the trader has survived market scrutiny — but check per-follower profits too
- ⏱️ **Time as a trader**: Traders with 6+ months on the books who have survived at least one boom-and-bust cycle are more trustworthy

### 3.2 Pitfall Checklist

- ❌ Picking based on 7-day returns alone → Short-term luck plays a massive role
- ❌ Sky-high returns with an equally huge drawdown → A high-leverage gambler; blowing up is only a matter of time
- ❌ Very low trade frequency (a few trades a month) → Weak replication effect, minimal rebate and profits
- ❌ Set it and forget it → Traders go through hot and cold streaks; review the data at least monthly

> 💡 **Sound strategy**: Copy 2–3 traders with different styles at the same time (trend + scalping + conservative) to spread the risk of any single trader having a bad run.
