## 3. Layered Liquidation: Insurance Fund & ADL

Bybit’s liquidation process is tiered, not a binary "liquidate everything" switch:
1.  **Cancel Open Orders:** Frees up margin immediately.
2.  **Step-Down Risk Limits:** Reduces required maintenance margin gradually to minimize trade size before a full liquidation.
3.  **Bankruptcy Price Takeover:** If MMR hits 100%, the system takes over at the bankruptcy price.
4.  **Insurance Fund:** If the executed price is worse than the bankruptcy price (i.e., the trader owes money), the **Insurance Fund** covers the deficit. No "socialized losses" where winners are forced to pay for losers.
5.  **Auto-Deleveraging (ADL):** If the Insurance Fund is depleted, positions from profitable traders (ranked by PnL/Leverage) are automatically reduced to close the gap.

Compared to older exchanges where "wicks" cause instant 100% losses, Bybit’s framework is institutional-grade risk management.
