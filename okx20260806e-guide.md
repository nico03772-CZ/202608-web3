## Connecting to DApps Correctly

1.  Go to **[Discover]** → Search for a protocol or paste a URL → Click **[Connect Wallet]** → Select OKX Wallet.
2.  Confirm the **Network + Address** in the pop-up. Approval only allows the DApp to read your public key; it does not grant spending rights.
3.  Initiate Swap/Stake → Review Gas, Slippage, and Route hops → Sign.
4.  **Signing** uses your private key to verify the request. After broadcasting, check the TxID on a block explorer.
5.  Post-interaction, return to **[Approval Mgmt]** and reduce unused approval limits to 0 or revoke them entirely.

> **Red Flags:** DApps asking you to paste your seed phrase, requesting "Unlimited Approvals" while rushing you to sign, or using phishing URLs (e.g., `okx-wallet.com`). Disconnect immediately.
