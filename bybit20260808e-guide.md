## 1. Liquidation Isn't "Zero at Mark Price": The Triple Safety Net

Most exchanges use binary liquidation logic: Mark Price hits Threshold → Market Sell-off → Socialized Loss (winners pay for losers). Bybit employs a tiered approach:

2.  **Step-Down Risk Limits:** Larger positions occupy higher Maintenance Margin brackets. Before a full liquidation, the system progressively reduces position size to lower margin requirements. It doesn't nuke the entire position at once.
3.  **Insurance Fund:** If the price crashes through the Bankruptcy Price, the **Insurance Fund** covers the deficit. There is **no "Socialized Loss" mechanism**—profitable traders are not forced to subsidize losers.
4.  **Auto-Deleveraging (ADL):** If the fund is depleted, positions are automatically closed against counterparties ranked by "Profit × Leverage." The ranking is transparent and predictable, not random de-ranking.
